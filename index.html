Scale Drawing Studio

Math 2201, Unit 6: Proportional Reasoning (indicators 6.1, 6.2, 6.3, 6.4, 6.12)

Every scale diagram follows one definition, stated in words: k = diagram measurement divided by actual measurement. First measure a diagram and state its scale factor. Then draw your own diagram to a stated scale on the grid canvas. This is practice only, and nothing you enter leaves this page.

Drag the ruler so its zero lines up with one end of a side, then read the value where the other end falls. The ruler's centimetres are drawn to match the diagram, so they work at any screen size. You can also click the ruler and move it with the arrow keys (Shift moves it faster). Rotate it to measure the vertical side.
